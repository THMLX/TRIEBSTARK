PERSONAL / THE FIRST NOTE

A small place
on a big internet.

THMLX   /   27 September 2026   /   2 MIN READ

A personal website doesn't need to explain everything about a person. Sometimes, it just needs to feel like a place they can call their own.

That's the idea behind TRIEBSTARK. A small, independent space on an internet that can feel very crowded. Somewhere for a thought, a project, a useful link, or something that doesn't quite fit anywhere else.

There is something appealing about starting with a simple page and an open question: what belongs here? The answer can change. A website can grow at the same pace as the person behind it.

A home, with room to grow.

This first version brings together a short introduction, a link to my GitHub profile, and this note. A modest beginning, and a deliberate one. The point is to have a place to return to and something to build on.

The design follows that thought: warm colours, generous space, and a ribbon that refuses to take a straight line. A little structure, with space left open.

Consider this the beginning.

For now, have a look around. You can explore the source of this website, or find my public profile on GitHub.

Thanks for stopping by this little corner.

— THMLX
