01 / PLAYSTYLE & CLASSES

Four classes.
More than one perspective.

My PvP is not tied to one class or one format. I play Hunter, Warrior, Rogue, and Druid — from duels and open-world fights to battlegrounds and group PvP.

Playing different classes means looking at the same fight from different sides: positioning, pressure, control, and the decision to commit or reset. That is the perspective I bring to working through a setup.

Hunter

03 / CONTROL

Rogue

04 / ADAPTABILITY

02 / GEAR SETUPS

Make the pieces fit.

Gear choices and upgrade paths that account for what you own, what you can realistically obtain, and the kind of PvP you play.

Bring: a gear list or screenshots.

03 / STRATEGY

Work through the fight.

Matchup preparation, positioning, cooldown decisions, and a plan for situations that keep giving you trouble.

Bring: a matchup or a specific scenario.

04 / MACROS

Controls with a purpose.

Macros adapted to your bindings and game client, with an explanation of what each one does and how to use it.

Bring: the action you want to simplify.

05 / ADDONS

See what matters.

Addon choices and interface setups focused on the information you need during a fight, with compatibility checked for your version.

Bring: a screenshot of your interface.

06 / COACHING

Turn a fight into feedback.

Focused coaching around the decisions and habits you want to improve. A clip, a recurring problem, or a clear goal is a good starting point.

Bring: a clip or a question.

04 / KEEP IT SIMPLE

Start with
one useful question.

You do not need a perfect brief. A little context is enough to start a conversation.

  1. 01

    Tell me what you play.

    Classic or Forever, your class/spec, and the kind of PvP you enjoy. Include your current setup if it matters to the question.

  2. 02

    Give me something specific.

    A matchup you struggle with, a gearing choice, a UI problem, or a clip you want to understand.

  3. 03

    We work out the next step.

    We can talk through the right format and scope, whether that is a focused answer, a tailored setup, or coaching.
