TRIEBSTARK   /   SEPTEMBER 2026

A personal site for Thomalex, built around a simple idea: give curiosity a home.

The design.

Deep green-charcoal tones, orange accents, and a mix of serif and sans-serif type. Dark mode is the default; the sun or moon button in the header switches to a warm paper light theme and back. Fonts come from your device, so there is no external font request. The gym image is an AI-enhanced interpretation of a photograph supplied by Thomalex, featuring a chalk-covered hand and a rack of dumbbells. The World of Warcraft character images on the gaming page were supplied by Thomalex and are displayed without AI alteration.

The Arnold Schwarzenegger training photograph is by Harry Chase / Los Angeles Times, from the UCLA Library collection, via Wikimedia Commons. It is used under CC BY 4.0 and resized for display. It appears as an archival bodybuilding reference.

The build.

These pages use HTML, CSS, and a small amount of JavaScript for the theme and language toggles, mobile menu, and copy-link button. The website is hosted on Spaceship Hyperlift, and its source is available in the TRIEBSTARK GitHub repository. The site was created with AI assistance.

Privacy, plainly.

This site's code does not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or a contact form. It does not ask you to create an account or submit personal information.

Your theme and language choices are saved in this browser using local storage, so they stay the same when you return or open another page. These preferences stay on your device and are not sent to us. If browser storage is unavailable, the toggles still work for the current page.

Like other web hosting services, the hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver and operate the site. External profile links take you to other services with their own privacy practices.

Copy buttons write only the displayed username or this website's address to your clipboard when you click them. They do not read your clipboard.

A site that works for more people.

The pages include keyboard navigation, visible focus styles, a skip-to-content link, and layouts for smaller screens. Motion is reduced when your device requests it. Most of the site works without JavaScript.
