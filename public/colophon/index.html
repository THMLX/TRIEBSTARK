TRIEBSTARK   /   SEPTEMBER 2026

A personal site for THMLX, built around a simple idea: give curiosity a home.

The design.

Warm paper tones, orange accents, and a mix of serif and sans-serif type. Fonts come from your device, so there is no external font request. The orange ribbon artwork was created for this site using AI image generation; it is an illustration, not a photograph of a physical sculpture.

The build.

These pages use HTML, CSS, and a small amount of JavaScript for the mobile menu and copy-link button. The website is hosted on Spaceship Hyperlift, and its source is available in the TRIEBSTARK GitHub repository. The site was created with AI assistance.

Privacy, plainly.

This site's code does not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or a contact form. It does not ask you to create an account or submit personal information.

Like other web hosting services, the hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver and operate the site. External links, including GitHub, take you to other services with their own privacy practices.

The copy-link button writes only this website's address to your clipboard when you click it. It does not read your clipboard.

A site that works for more people.

The pages include keyboard navigation, visible focus styles, a skip-to-content link, and layouts for smaller screens. Motion is reduced when your device requests it. Most of the site works without JavaScript.

For the person behind this space, visit THMLX on GitHub.
