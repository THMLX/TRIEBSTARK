Lifting weights and bodybuilding are a real passion of mine. I enjoy the physical effort, but also the attention to detail: how a movement feels, how a physique develops, and how individual sessions become part of something longer.

The classic bodybuilding imagery is part of the appeal. The Arnold photograph above captures the training behind the physique — an aspect of that era I wanted to make room for here.
